Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

1. Summary

Door Tally is a door-to-door sales tracker. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your daily counts: doors, opens, pitches, sales, lost-sale reasons, notes and callbacks (addresses or names you type).
  • Your goal: money goal, amount per deal and deadline.
  • Voice recordings: recordings you make in the Record tab, with your tags and notes. They are saved only on your device and are never uploaded. You are responsible for getting everyone's consent before recording a conversation where the law requires it.

3. What is shared on the leaderboard

To run the shared leaderboard, the app stores the following in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database, where every Door Tally user can see it: the name you enter, your money goal, your amount per deal, today's and all-time door and deal counts, and a timestamped entry each time you log a deal. It is stored under a random id, not your Apple ID. Callbacks, addresses, notes and recordings are never shared.

4. Service providers

Apple, through iCloud, stores the leaderboard data. Nobody else receives your data.

5. Your choices

You can change the name shown on the board at any time. "Erase all my counts" in settings clears your counts on the phone and resets your leaderboard numbers. To have your leaderboard entry removed completely, contact us and we will delete it within thirty days.

6. Children

Door Tally is meant for adults working in sales and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
