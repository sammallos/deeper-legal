Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

1. Summary

Deeper collects the minimum needed to run your training. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. What we collect

  • Profile you enter: optional name, training goal, experience level, training days, activity level, height and weight. Height and weight are used only to size workouts.
  • Training data: sessions, holds, contractions you log, streaks, experience points, ranks, badges and lessons read.
  • Account data: if you sign in with Apple or Google, an identifier from that provider and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address.
  • Purchase data: subscription status and an anonymous app user id, processed by RevenueCat so Deeper Pro works and can be restored. We never see your card details; Apple handles payment.
  • Buddies: when you share a dive card or add a friend's code, your diver name, best hold, level, streak and this week's session count are stored under a random id in Apple's CloudKit public database so friends who hold your code can see them. Reset all progress clears that record.
  • Heart rate: the camera pulse check processes the camera image on your device in real time; no image, video or reading is stored or sent anywhere. With your permission, the app can read heart rate from Apple Health during sessions and write finished sessions to Health as Mindful Minutes. Both are off until you switch them on in the Cabin, and Health data never leaves your phone. Health data is never used for marketing or advertising, never shared with third parties, and never sold, as Apple's rules require.
  • Device settings: your theme, sound and reminder preferences, and your acceptance of the Safety Notice with its date and version.

3. Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your device. If you sign in, your profile and training data are also stored with your account so they follow you to a new phone. The web preview of Deeper stores the same data with the account you used to open it.

4. What is shared and with whom

  • Other users: only if you share a dive card or crew code, and then only your entered name, best hold, rank and streak. If you opt in to the global board the same fields are visible there.
  • Service providers: Apple and Google for sign-in and payment, RevenueCat for subscription management. Each processes only what it needs and under its own privacy terms.
  • Nobody else. We do not share, rent or sell personal data.

5. Your choices and rights

You can change or clear your name, sign out, or delete everything with "Reset all progress" in the Cabin, which removes local data and your account record. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data and to object to processing; contact us through the App Store support link and we will respond within thirty days.

6. Retention

Data is kept while you use Deeper and until you delete it. Account data is deleted when you reset progress or ask us to delete it. Purchase records are kept as long as required for the subscription and by law.

7. Children

Deeper is for adults 18 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used it, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device locked and your sign-in private.

9. Changes

We will show material changes to this policy in the app. The version date appears below.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: use the support link on the App Store listing or the contact form at sammellows.com/contact.
